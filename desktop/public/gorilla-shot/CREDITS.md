# Gorilla Shot

Adapted from [Underrun](https://github.com/phoboslab/underrun), the 2018 js13k game by Dominic Szablewski. Original concept, graphics, and programming: Dominic Szablewski. Original music: Andreas Lösch. Underrun is MIT licensed; the license notice is in `LICENSE.md`.

The bundled Sonant-X source is a modified version of work by Nicolas Vanhoren, Marcus Geelnard, and Jake Taylor under the zlib license. Its copyright and license notice remains at the top of `source/sonantx-reduced.js`.

Gorilla Shot adds GorillaPunch branding, a new HUD, score and survival tracking, pause and restart controls, and local/account progress sync. The Underrun source files in `source/` are included with their original structure and any modifications are specific to this adaptation.
